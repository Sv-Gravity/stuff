ANTAGONIST

You're a stickman the size of an ant. Haul the giant food home together. One of you is a traitor.

Workers: explore the yard, find the big food, grab it (Space) and push toward the nest. Everyone's push adds up, so one ant pulling the wrong way slows the whole team. Bring crumbs home for extra time.
Scents: when someone finds the food, a scent arrow points everyone to it. Traitors can plant fake scents (T) that look identical, until an honest ant gets close and sniffs out the lie.
Traitor: you always know where the food is. Grab on and drag it the wrong way, trip ants off it (Q), eat crumbs (Q). Run out the clock.
Accuse: stand next to an ant and press V. If a majority accuses the same ant they get webbed. Wrong guess and the accusers lose their vote for the round.
Antennae meeting (M): once per round per ant, freeze everyone to talk and vote. The clock keeps running.
After each round a replay shows who really pushed which way. First side to 3 points wins.
